# AlTar 2: a Bayesian framework for inverse problems

AlTar samples the posterior distribution of inverse problems with Markov chain Monte Carlo methods,
on cpus and GPUs, from a workstation to a cluster. It is named after the physicist
[Albert Tarantola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Tarantola), for his pioneering work on
the Bayesian view of inverse problems.
